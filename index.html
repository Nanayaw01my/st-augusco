About AUGUSCO

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Established in 1957, St. Augustine's Senior High School — affectionately known as AUGUSCO — is a distinguished mixed-gender Catholic school located in Bogoso, in the Western Region of Ghana.

Originally founded as the teacher training section of St. Augustine's College, Cape Coast, the school was relocated and transformed into a secondary institution under the guidance of the Catholic Church. By 1973 it had fully evolved into the dynamic senior high school it is today.

We are committed to the holistic development of every student — academically, morally, and socially — in an environment grounded in our three core values.

Admissions

Begin Your AUGUSCO Journey

Admission into St. Augustine's SHS is primarily through the Computerised School Selection & Placement System (CSSPS). Prospective students may also enquire directly.

1

Complete BECE

Sit and pass the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) conducted by WAEC.

2

CSSPS Selection

Select AUGUSCO as one of your school choices on the Ghana Education Service's CSSPS portal.

3

Check Placement

Log onto the CSSPS website to check your placement result and confirm your offer.

4

Report to School

Arrive on the official reporting date with all required documents, fees, and personal effects.
